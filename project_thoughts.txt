4) Really important: Need to have a "live" analysis. Not just "What did the price say Monday market open". I need this option helper to work no matter what time of the week. Sunday afternoon, Monday before market, Monday after 9:30am, Monday after 4, Tuesday Morning, etc. This helper should be able to work at any time, and the end goal is to simulate the closing on Friday. So this option helper needs to use the current date (e.g. Wednesday) and create a Friday spread based off the current day + current week's prices.

6) Saw an interesting point from hedge funds, use Markov chain (only care about what your current state is, to determine the possible next states) to better determine the next state (monto carelo simulation).
